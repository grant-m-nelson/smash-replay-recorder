Smash Replay Recorder records every replay on your Switch as its own video.

To press buttons on your Switch over Bluetooth, setup makes these changes to this PC:

  - Installs usbipd-win (USB device sharing), if it isn't already installed.

  - Turns on Windows Subsystem for Linux (WSL), if it isn't already on.
    Windows may need to restart once.

  - Adds a small Windows service, "Smash Replay Recorder Bluetooth". While the
    recorder is open it lends your Bluetooth adapter to the recorder, and it
    gives it back to Windows when you close the recorder. Bluetooth headphones
    or mice won't work while the recorder is open.

  - The first time the recorder runs, it adds its own Linux helper to WSL,
    named "SmashRecorder".

Uninstalling removes the service and the Linux helper. usbipd-win and WSL stay
installed because other programs may use them; you can remove them from
Windows Settings > Apps.

Nothing on your Switch is changed or deleted.
